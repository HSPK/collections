Cartographic scope and limitations:
This is generalized cartography at a nominal 1:50,000,000 source scale, not
a surveyed map, navigation chart, topographic model, or cadastral boundary
dataset. Zoom enlarges the same fixed-resolution coastline; it does not
download higher-resolution tiles. Inland water is only represented where
the land dataset contains a hole (not a complete lakes/rivers layer).

The observer picker and Earth texture use the same land polygons, including
islands, polygon holes, and antimeridian cuts. Coordinates are CRS84
[longitude, latitude] in degrees, east/north positive. The display is
equirectangular (Plate carree), north at top: x=(longitude+180)/360 and
y=(90-latitude)/180. It exaggerates polar area and is not equal-area.
Antimeridian cuts are filled, but not drawn as physical coastlines.
The source Antarctic closure is at approximately -89.998926 degrees;
the original coordinates are retained rather than claiming surveyed pole data.

The textured Earth is a mean-radius display sphere. Physical observer
calculations elsewhere use a geodetic ellipsoid; this map and texture do
not replace or alter that model. No elevation, terrain, political borders,
surface imagery, or topographic accuracy is implied.

This file and its attribution are bundled offline and served same-origin.
There are no runtime external map requests, remote tiles, or tracking.
